# Agora Controller
> Agora Controller requires a dedictaed control port on at least 1 network switch to function.  This control port is connected directly to the VLAN ID=256 network.  Reccomend last port on rear.
{.is-info}


> IGMP Querier is not automatically elected.  You must manually choose the querier switch for EVERY VLAN with IGMP snooping enabled.
{.is-warning}

# H1

- H1 is a "something" to Dante and Vice Versa converter, with a network switch built in.  
- Ideal situation for it is as a network endpoint

> H1 is a hybrid platform mounted in a standard 19-inch half-rack of one unit height dedicated to the multiplexing, transport and control of signals and the delivery of professional AoIP protocols.
> In stand-alone use, H1 therefore behaves like a two-way audio bridge allowing Dante streams from the internal core to be converted into all the formats of the AxC cards present in the Auvitran brand catalog. As an extension, H1 provides the dual functionality of transport network node and termination point for the delivery and removal of audio signals.
>
